About SportConnect

SportConnect is a mobile application designed for sports enthusiasts in Vietnam. It lets you discover and instantly book badminton, basketball, football, pickleball, and other courts at venues across Ho Chi Minh City and beyond.

Beyond court booking, SportConnect connects you with local events, coaching sessions, and a community of players who share your passion for sport — all from one app.
